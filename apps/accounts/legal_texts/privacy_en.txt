This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Qrumy ("Qrumy", "we", "us") collects when you use qrumy.com and the Qrumy products (the "Service"), why we collect it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. We collect as little data as we need and we do not sell personal data.

## 1. Who is responsible
- For data about our users (people who sign up and work in Qrumy), Qrumy is the controller.
- For data that our customers collect through Qrumy from their own customers, respondents, visitors and staff (for example survey answers, QR scans or fault reports), the customer organization is the controller and Qrumy is a processor acting on its instructions. If you answered a survey or scanned a QR code of a business, please contact that business about your data. We will help them answer your request.

## 2. Data we collect about users
- Account data: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), language, two-factor authentication settings, and the date you accepted our Terms.
- Organization data: organization name, logo, team members and their roles, invitations, plan and usage.
- Content you create: QR codes, surveys, assets, documents, photos and other data you add.
- Security and activity data: an audit log of important actions in your organization (who did what and when), login events and the technical data needed to protect accounts.
- Sign-in with Google (optional): if you use it, we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier from Google.
- Communication: messages you send to support.

## 3. Data collected through our customers' QR codes, surveys and forms
- QR scans: time of the scan, device type, operating system, browser, approximate location (country and city, derived from the IP address) and whether the scan looks automated. The IP address is not stored: we convert it into a one-way, daily-changing hashed value used only to count unique scans.
- Survey responses: the answers given. Contact details (such as name, email or phone) are stored only when the survey asks for them and the person gives consent. We do not store respondents' IP addresses.
- Fault reports on assets: the description, priority, an optional photo and optional contact details given by the reporter.

## 4. Why we use data (and our legal bases)
- To provide the Service you asked for, including your account, organization and content (performance of a contract).
- To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse such as phishing QR codes and spam, and enforce our Terms (legitimate interests).
- To send service emails: account verification, password resets, invitations, notifications you enabled and important changes (performance of a contract and legitimate interests).
- To understand how the Service is used in aggregate and improve it (legitimate interests).
- To comply with legal obligations.
We do not use your content or your respondents' data to train AI models or for advertising, and we do not sell or rent personal data.

## 5. Cookies
We use only cookies that are necessary for the Service: a session cookie to keep you signed in, a security (CSRF) cookie, a cookie that remembers your language and a short-lived cookie that prevents a survey visit from being counted twice. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies, so no cookie banner is required. If we ever add optional cookies, we will ask for your consent first.

## 6. Service providers
We share personal data only with providers that help us run the Service, under contracts that require them to protect it:
- Hostinger: server hosting (our database and files are stored on servers we rent).
- Resend: delivery of transactional emails.
- Sentry (if enabled): error monitoring. We configure it not to send user details, request bodies or form contents.
- Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
We may also disclose data if required by law, to protect the rights and safety of people or of the Service, or as part of a merger or acquisition, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

## 7. International transfers
Our providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the European Union and the United States. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 8. How long we keep data
- Account and organization data: while the account or organization exists. When you delete them, the data is deleted from our live systems.
- Detailed QR scan records: 180 days. After that only aggregated daily totals are kept.
- Survey responses, assets and other content: until the customer deletes them or the organization is deleted.
- Audit log: while the organization exists. When a user deletes their account, their email in the audit log is replaced with an anonymous label.
- Backups: backups are kept in access-restricted storage for up to 14 days and then deleted automatically.

## 9. Security
We protect data with encryption in transit (HTTPS), hashed passwords, optional two-factor authentication, strict separation between organizations, role-based access, private file storage, audit logs and regular backups. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a personal-data breach where the law requires it.

## 10. Your rights
Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can update your profile and delete your account yourself in your account settings. For other requests, write to privacy@qrumy.com. We will answer within the time required by law (normally within one month). You also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

## 11. Children
The Service is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data from user sign-ups. Customers must not use Qrumy surveys to collect children's data without the consent required by law.

## 12. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. The current version, with its version number and date, is always on this page. We will notify you in advance of material changes by email or in the Service.

## 13. Contact
Privacy questions and requests: privacy@qrumy.com. General support: support@qrumy.com.
